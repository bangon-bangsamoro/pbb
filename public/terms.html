2. Who we are

This Site is operated by Partido Bangon Bangsamoro ("PBB," "we," "us," or "our"), a regional political party organized under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines and registered to contest elections within the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM).

3. Using this website

The Site provides information about PBB's platform of government, candidates, events, and ways to get involved with the 2026 Bangsamoro Parliamentary Elections campaign. You may browse the Site and use its public features without creating an account. Certain areas (such as the staff dashboard) require sign-in and are restricted to authorized PBB personnel and volunteers.
